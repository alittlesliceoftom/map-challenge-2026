# Waterloo Bridge, given back

I love cycling and walking, and Oxford Circus going car-free feels like it should be the start, not the finish. So for Utopia I picked the walk I would most like to see handed back: out of Waterloo station, over Waterloo Bridge, past the Strand and up into Covent Garden.

In this version there are no cars. The old carriageway on the bridge, Lancaster Place and the Strand is now just more pavement, so people drift diagonally across it the way they do in a piazza instead of queuing at crossings. Zoomed out you see their trails; zoom in past street level and they turn into little walking figures.

It is less of a fantasy than it looks. The Strand by St Mary le Strand has been car-free since the Strand Aldwych scheme in 2022, and the Golden Jubilee footbridges next door already prove the river crossing works on foot.

Buses are the honest compromise. Most routes divert via Blackfriars and Westminster, and what is left runs through a single signalled lane down the middle of the bridge, shared with bikes. Traffic lights at each end let a short convoy through one way, then the other, so most of the time the lane is just a cycle path.

Everyone on the map is made up: the trips are generated, not counted, and the numbers are meant to feel like a busy Saturday rather than to measure anything. Next step: a "now" version with today's traffic, and a slider to swipe between the two.
