# Phones got there first

In Kenya, mobile money reached places the power grid never did. In the 2022 DHS, around 9 in 10 adults in almost every county use a phone for financial transactions, while household electricity ranges from 16% in Turkana to 95% in Nairobi. Bank accounts lag far behind both, at about a quarter of adults in the typical county.

The colours split each measure into county thirds, so "low" mobile money still means roughly 90%. The pink counties have more mobile money than their electricity would suggest, and the blue ones have both. The orange lines are the high-voltage grid, and the counties they miss, such as Turkana, Marsabit, Mandera and Wajir, are the least electrified in the country.
